Privacy Policy — Mobile Mechanic Shop OS

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS is an automotive service management application developed and published by Revive N Drive. This Privacy Policy explains how Mobile Mechanic Shop OS accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

Information You Enter Into the App

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS allows users to enter and manage information related to their automotive service business. This may include customer names, phone numbers and other contact information; vehicle information such as year, make, model, mileage and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN); repair orders and job information; inspection results; service history; notes; photographs; signatures; and business information entered into the application's settings.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have appropriate permission to enter and store information concerning their customers.

Camera and Photos

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS may request access to the device camera and/or photographs when the user chooses features that require them. Camera access may be used to take vehicle or repair photographs and to scan vehicle information such as VIN barcodes.

Photo access may be used to select, save, or manage images associated with jobs, vehicles, reports, or other app functionality. These permissions are used only when required for user-requested application features.

How Information Is Used

Information entered into Mobile Mechanic Shop OS is used to provide the application's automotive service-management functions, including managing customers and vehicles, creating and maintaining repair orders, documenting repairs and inspections, maintaining service history, creating customer reports, and related business-management functions.

Data Storage and Transmission

The current version of Mobile Mechanic Shop OS primarily stores application records locally on the user's device. Users should protect access to their device and maintain appropriate backups of important business records.

Features may be added in future versions that provide accounts, cloud synchronization, backups, subscriptions, payment functionality, or other online services. This Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated as necessary before such functionality is made available.

Sharing of Information

Revive N Drive does not sell personal information entered into Mobile Mechanic Shop OS.

Information may leave the application when a user deliberately uses a feature intended to export, save, or share information, such as saving a report or photograph to the device or sharing information through another application or service selected by the user.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to design Mobile Mechanic Shop OS so that information is handled securely. However, no device, software application, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices, device credentials, backups, and any information they export from the application.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Mobile Mechanic Shop OS remains on the user's device until it is deleted through available application functions, cleared from application storage, or the application and its associated data are removed from the device, subject to Android's applicable storage and backup behavior.

The application provides functionality for managing and deleting applicable customer, vehicle, and job records.

If future versions introduce server-based accounts or cloud storage, appropriate mechanisms for requesting deletion of applicable account and cloud data will be provided and this policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS is intended for automotive professionals and other users managing automotive service work. It is not designed or directed toward children.

Third-Party Services

Certain device or platform functionality used by the application may be provided through Android, Google Play, or other technology providers. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies when their services are used.

Any future third-party services or SDKs that materially affect the application's collection, processing, or sharing of user data will be reflected in this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Mobile Mechanic Shop OS develops and new functionality is introduced. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
