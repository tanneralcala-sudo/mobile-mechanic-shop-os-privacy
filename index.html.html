Privacy Policy — Mobile Mechanic Shop OS

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS is an automotive service management application developed and published by Revive N Drive. This Privacy Policy explains how Mobile Mechanic Shop OS accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

Information You Enter Into the App

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS allows users to enter and manage information related to their automotive service business. This may include customer names, phone numbers and other contact information; vehicle information such as year, make, model, mileage and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN); repair orders and job information; inspection results; service history; notes; photographs; signatures; and business information entered into the application's settings.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have appropriate permission to enter and store information concerning their customers.

Camera and Photos

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS may request access to the device camera and/or photographs when the user chooses features that require them. Camera access may be used to take vehicle or repair photographs and to scan vehicle information such as VIN barcodes.

Photo access may be used to select, save, or manage images associated with jobs, vehicles, reports, or other app functionality. These permissions are used only when required for user-requested application features.

How Information Is Used

Information entered into Mobile Mechanic Shop OS is used to provide the application's automotive service-management functions, including managing customers and vehicles, creating and maintaining repair orders, documenting repairs and inspections, maintaining service history, creating customer reports, and related business-management functions.

Accounts, Cloud Storage, and Synchronization

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS uses a local-first design. Application records may be stored on the user's device and, when cloud features are enabled, synchronized with secure backend services provided through Supabase. Cloud-synchronized information may include account information, customers, customer addresses and contact information, vehicles, repair orders and jobs, notes, service history, settings, templates, appointments, photographs, attachments, and related application records.

Cloud synchronization is used to provide backup, restoration, multi-device access, account-based data access, and synchronization of supported application records. Photographs and attachments selected for cloud-enabled features may be uploaded to private cloud storage associated with the user's account.

Users should continue to protect access to their devices and account credentials and maintain appropriate copies of important business records.

Google Sign-In

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS may allow users to authenticate using Google Sign-In through Supabase Authentication. When a user chooses Google Sign-In, information made available for authentication, such as the user's Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information authorized by the user, may be processed to create and maintain the user's Mobile Mechanic Shop OS account.

Google Calendar Integration

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS offers optional Google Calendar synchronization for users who choose to connect a Google account. When enabled, the application may access and process Google Calendar information necessary to provide two-way appointment synchronization, including event titles, descriptions, dates and times, event identifiers, and service-address or location information associated with scheduled work.

Google Calendar data is accessed and used only to provide user-requested scheduling and synchronization functionality. The application does not sell Google user data or use Google Calendar data for advertising. Users may disconnect Calendar integration in the application where available and may revoke the application's Google access through their Google Account permissions.

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing of Information

Revive N Drive does not sell personal information entered into Mobile Mechanic Shop OS.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate requested application functionality, including Supabase for authentication, database, storage, synchronization, and server-side functions, and Google when a user chooses Google Sign-In or Google Calendar integration. These services process information as necessary to provide their respective functions.

Information may also leave the application when a user deliberately uses a feature intended to export, save, or share information, such as saving a report or photograph to the device or sharing information through another application or service selected by the user.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to design Mobile Mechanic Shop OS so that information is handled securely. However, no device, software application, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices, device credentials, backups, and any information they export from the application.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Mobile Mechanic Shop OS remains on the user's device until it is deleted through available application functions, cleared from application storage, or the application and its associated data are removed from the device, subject to Android's applicable storage and backup behavior.

The application provides functionality for managing and deleting applicable customer, vehicle, and job records. When cloud synchronization is enabled, supported deletions may also be synchronized to the user's cloud data.

Users may request deletion of their account and applicable cloud-stored data through the account-deletion process provided by Mobile Mechanic Shop OS or by contacting Revive N Drive at the email address below. Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate obligations.

Revoking Google access stops future authorized access to Google data but does not by itself delete unrelated Mobile Mechanic Shop OS records that the user previously created or stored. Users may separately delete applicable app records or request account deletion.

Children's Privacy

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS is intended for automotive professionals and other users managing automotive service work. It is not designed or directed toward children.

Third-Party Services

Mobile Mechanic Shop OS uses or may interact with third-party services required to provide application functionality, including Android and Google Play services, Supabase for backend infrastructure and authentication, Google Sign-In and Google Calendar APIs for optional connected features, and RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies when their services are used.

Subscription-related information may be processed to determine purchase status and premium feature entitlement. Mobile Mechanic Shop OS does not receive or store a user's full payment-card details from Google Play purchases.

Material changes to third-party services or SDKs that affect the application's collection, processing, or sharing of user data will be reflected in this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Mobile Mechanic Shop OS develops and new functionality is introduced. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
